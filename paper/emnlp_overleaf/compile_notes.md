For final submission, replace the fallback `acl.sty` with the official ACL/EMNLP style file from the current author kit and use the official bibliography style.

Short-paper target checked against official EMNLP/ACL guidance on May 4, 2026: main-conference short papers are 4 pages of content, with references and appendices outside the limit; accepted short papers receive one additional content page for final revisions. This draft is intended for that 4-page short-paper shape, but exact page count requires Overleaf compilation.
